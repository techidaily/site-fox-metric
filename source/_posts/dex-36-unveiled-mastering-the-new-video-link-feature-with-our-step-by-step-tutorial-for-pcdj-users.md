## DEX 3.6 Unveiled: Mastering the New 'Video Link' Feature with Our Step-by-Step Tutorial for PCDJ Users

[![Video Link Feature in DEX 3.6 DJ and Video Mixing Software](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/videolink-coverimage.jpg?resize=530%2C298&ssl=1)](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/videolink-coverimage.jpg?fit=530%2C298&ssl=1 "DEX 3 DJ and Video Mixing software Video Link")

[DEX 3.6 (BETA) was published for public beta](https://pcdj.com/dex-3-6-beta-2-now-available/) last week and introduced a **new ‘Video Link’ Feature**. We demonstrate how this feature can be utilized during **video mix sets** in the video demonstration below.

VDJ’s **mixing music videos** with DEX 3 can now display videos (stock video, visuals or even other music videos) when mixing regular audio tracks.

[VIEW LARGER IMAGE ![DEX 3 Video Link Feature Setting](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/videolinkfeature.jpg?fit=300%2C300&ssl=1 "DEX 3 Video Link Feature Setting")](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/videolinkfeature.jpg?fit=1030%2C1030&ssl=1)

**In DEX 3.6’s options you’ll find a new tab for ‘Video’.** Under the new tab you’ll find a section titled ‘**Link videos to audio tracks**‘ — here you’ll find the settings to ‘**Automatically link videos to audio tracks**‘ and the option to select the directory of videos you wish DEX 3 to automatically select from. Another option is to ‘**Randomly select videos**‘, instead of playing through the directory in alphabetical or numerical order (depending on how your videos are titled).

We think **this is a huge addition to DEX 3 for VDJ’s** (video DJs) that want to **incorporate audio tracks into their video mixing sets**. While [BPM Supreme](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) offers an in-depth selection of **HD music videos** in all genres, the availability of music videos for all the latest tracks pales in comparison to what DJs can currently find strictly in audio format.
