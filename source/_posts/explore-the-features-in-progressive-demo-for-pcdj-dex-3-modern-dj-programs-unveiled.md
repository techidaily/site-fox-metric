## Explore the Features in Progressive Demo for PCDJ DEX 3 - Modern DJ Programs Unveiled

[![](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/djmixingsoftware-dex2preview.jpg?resize=640%2C270&ssl=1)](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/djmixingsoftware-dex2preview.jpg?fit=640%2C400&ssl=1 "djmixingsoftware-dex2preview")

  
**DJ Mixing Software: PCDJ DEX 2** 

_**DEX 2 is our top mixing software solution for DJs that want to offer the total multimedia experience to their clients and patrons.**_  A true all-in-one [DJ mixing software](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) solution that blends audio, music videos and karaoke seamlessly. With one-click beat sync, high-quality time stretching, support for 60+ [DJ controllers](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) and various effects/transitions [PCDJ DEX 2](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) is truly complete product for the pro DJ. 

For the last 8+ months we've been working on a major platform upgrade for both of our [DJ software](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) products, DEX 2 and RED mobile 2 (which will consequently be called DEX 3 and RED Mobile 3). This is what’s called a “core upgrade” (Think Foundation) and includes a new graphics engine, new video mixing engine and brand new state-of-the-art browser. For the most part, our existing (it just sounds better) audio engine is staying put, with some minor tweaks and updates. 

We plan to publicly launch version 3 of both of our [DJ mixing software](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) products in the next month or so. Until then, since our customers have been very patient and we’d like to throw everyone a bone – – here are a few noteworthy new features and changes to expect in our upcoming version 3 of DEX. (**NOTE: FREE DJ software upgrades will be provided to all existing PCDJ DEX 2 and RED Mobile 2 users!**) 

  
**DEX 2 Graphics Engine Updates:**  

* Transparency on graphics is possible throughout the skin
* Controls over/inside other controls, z-order, non-standard shapes, masking, etc are now possible
* Retina support for the latest Macs (with dynamic on-off when needed)
* Dynamic resize the skin (to fit displays our skins don’t natively support)
* Sound display rendering greatly improved (RMS waveform + rhythm waveform overlapped)
* Sound display (including waveform) blinks when the track is close to the end (<30 seconds left)
* Truly separate video previews for each DJ deck (decks with fx preview, master mix preview)
* New Setting to enable/disable resetting of pitch after auto-mix

  
**DJ Controllers Engine Upgraded:** 

* The level of depth and layered support for DJ controllers using our DJ software has never been better!

**Browser Upgrades:** 

* The database is not a simple list (as in DEX 2) — all tracks browsed in the software are inserted into the database and they be instantly searched/used later
* Album artwork in lists/explorer (and optionally over the jog-wheels)
* Track tags are always displayed in the browser (even when browsing disk folders!)
* All-New "Side List" or Request List – double click, drag and drop, or click enter on your keyboard to send track to visble side list (which can be used for manual or Auto-Mix)

  
_**…And loads of other bug fixes and performance enhancements!**_ Once the first versions of DEX 3 and RED Mobile 3 are launched publicly, our users can expect regular updates to both[DJ software](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) platforms throughout this year. The new core upgrade will allow us to build on top of the code like never before possible – _**so very exciting times ahead!**_
